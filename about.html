Full-service renewable energy partner in Maharashtra

Leader — Powers & Renewable Energy Solutions is a full-service renewable energy company based in Babhaleshwar, Maharashtra. We design, supply and install solar rooftop systems for residential, commercial, and industrial clients. Our services also include solar water pumps, solar water heaters, wind turbines, biogas systems, logistics management, and complete project execution.
